# Still for Android

Open the [latest Android release](https://github.com/eerihyppanen/Still/releases/latest) on your phone and download the APK under **Assets**. Open it and choose **Update** if Still is already installed. Updating in place preserves the habits and check-ins stored on your phone. Uninstalling first erases that data.

Version 1.4.1 includes recurring Android reminders after a pause or a completed weekly goal. It also keeps eligible reminders when the local date moves back before a pause, puts unfinished habits first in the widget, and keeps old scheduled-day streaks in history when you switch to a weekly goal.

Each release includes a SHA-256 checksum for its APK. See the [verification record](../VERIFICATION.md) for test and emulator results.
